GlitchTip

Simple, open source error tracking

Sentry is no longer open source. We want a quality open source product to pick up where it left off, so we're making GlitchTip.

Subscribe to our newsletter to receive updates about our progress.

Want to know more?

GlitchTip is compatible with the Sentry API, so it can use their SDKs to receive error data from your application.
